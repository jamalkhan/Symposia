Node Services

        
A node runs zero, one or many Node Services. Each service is staked, scored, penalized and paid on its own:
          the more services you run to earn, the more you stake. Every service's earnings breakdown is public, so you can see what you're
          turning on and what you get in return.

        
Turning a service on here starts it on this machine. Staking it on-chain is a separate step (coming to NodeHost).
